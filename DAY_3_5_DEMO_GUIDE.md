
# EARTHSHIELD — Day 3.5 Demonstration Guide

## Module

People Safety + Emergency Assistance

## Purpose

This module demonstrates how EARTHSHIELD can:

1. Detect a disaster event.
2. Define an impact radius.
3. Identify people inside that radius.
4. Generate simulated alerts.
5. Find nearby emergency facilities.
6. Check facility availability.
7. Display people, disasters and facilities on a map.
8. Separate live data from synthetic test data.

## Synthetic Data

The following datasets are fictional demonstration data:

- `data/demo/people_demo.json`
- `data/demo/emergency_facilities_demo.json`
- `data/demo/disaster_test_scenarios.json`

No real people's personal information is used.

## Demonstration Procedure

1. Open EARTHSHIELD.
2. Open `People Assistance`.
3. Select `Ahmedabad Earthquake Demonstration`.
4. Press `RUN DISASTER SCENARIO`.
5. Observe the synthetic affected people.
6. Select an affected demo person.
7. Observe nearby emergency facilities.
8. Inspect the map.
9. Open `Data Sources`.
10. Show the separation between live and synthetic data.

## Important

The synthetic people and facilities represent a software-testing environment.
